ERM Tutor-AI

An AI tutor for the Empirical Research Methods course. It answers from the course's own lecture materials using retrieval-augmented generation, shows students where each answer comes from, and responds like a tutor with reflection and follow-up questions.

Research pilot · NLP

Whose Inference?

The same sentence means different things to different readers. This pilot predicts how a specific reader will interpret a statistical statement from a personal knowledge base of what they know and believe, rewrites the statement so it is not misread, and looks inside a language model to see how it uses that knowledge.

  • Knowledge-graph retrieval raises reader-prediction accuracy from 42% to 61% (75% with GPT-4o)
  • RSA-style cooperative rephrasing with a simulated listener
  • Activation patching and steering on Qwen2.5-0.5B

Instructional design · M.Sc. Educational Technology

How to be SMART about your goals

A self-paced e-learning unit that teaches students to set SMART goals for their academic and everyday life. I designed the learning objectives, structure and activities, and built the unit as an interactive H5P book.

  • Clear learning objectives and a short theory section before practice
  • One section per SMART criterion: self-paced resources, then a task on that criterion
  • Ends with "Your turn!": learners write a SMART goal for their own life, then a summary
